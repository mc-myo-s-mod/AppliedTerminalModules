# Equipment Module

<ItemImage id="appliedterminalmodules:equipment_module" scale={3} />

Install this card in a [Myotus terminal card slot](index.md) to access your equipment while the terminal is open.

The panel shows **four armor slots**—helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots—and **one offhand slot**. These are your player's actual equipment slots. Placing or taking an item changes what you are wearing or holding immediately, subject to the normal equipment restrictions.

Removing the module card or closing the terminal leaves the equipment on your player. The card provides access to the slots; it does not store the equipment.

## Recipe

The shapeless recipe uses an AE2 Basic Card, an Iron Chestplate, a Shield and an AE2 Logic Processor.
