# Grindstone Module

<ItemImage id="appliedterminalmodules:grindstone_module" scale={3} />

Install this card in a [Myotus terminal card slot](index.md) to use a grindstone from the terminal.

## Grinding items

Use the grindstone to repair or disenchant items under its normal rules. Disenchanting returns experience as usual; curses are not removed. Take the result to complete the operation.

If you remove the module card or close the terminal, the remaining inputs are returned to your player inventory; items that do not fit are dropped.
