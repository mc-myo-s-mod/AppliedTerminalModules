# Charging Module

<ItemImage id="appliedterminalmodules:charging_module" scale={3} />

Install this card in a [Myotus terminal card slot](index.md) to charge compatible tools from the terminal.

## Charging tools

The module draws energy from the ME network and charges items that support AE2 or Forge Energy (FE) charging. Insert a compatible item in the charging slot while the terminal is connected to an available ME energy supply. Charging speed and power use depend on the item and network conditions.

Removing the card or closing the terminal returns the charging item to your inventory. Overflow is dropped.

## Recipe

The shapeless recipe uses an AE2 Advanced Card, an AE2 Charger and an AE2 Engineering Processor.
