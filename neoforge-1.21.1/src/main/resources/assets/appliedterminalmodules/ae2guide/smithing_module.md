# Smithing Module

<ItemImage id="appliedterminalmodules:smithing_module" scale={3} />

Install this card in a [Myotus terminal card slot](index.md) to use a smithing table from the terminal.

## Smithing

Place the template, base item and addition in their matching slots. Available upgrades and trims follow the world's smithing recipes. Smithing templates are consumed according to the normal recipe rules.

In JEI or EMI, use **Alt + the recipe transfer button** to fill this module with one recipe set. Inputs come from ME storage first, then your inventory. Take the result yourself to consume the inputs. The module must be installed and visible.

If you remove the module card or close the terminal, the remaining inputs are returned to your player inventory; items that do not fit are dropped.

## Recipe

The shapeless recipe uses an AE2 Basic Card and a Smithing Table.
